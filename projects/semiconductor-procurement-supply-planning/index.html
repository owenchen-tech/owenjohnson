Executive summary

This case study is in development. It will examine sourcing and supply planning decisions using clearly labeled data and assumptions. No supplier rankings or completed results are available yet.

Business Problem

How can supplier selection and order allocation support production requirements while accounting for cost, quality, delivery, and shortage risk?

Background

The scope will connect procurement decisions with demand, inventory, and production requirements.

Methodology

Planned approach: define demand requirements, document data sources and assumptions, compare suppliers, assess supply gaps, and evaluate allocation scenarios.
